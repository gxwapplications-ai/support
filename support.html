Get in touch: gxwapplications@gmail.com Frequently Asked Questions How do I switch between lotteries? Use the EuroMillions, The Lotto, or Thunderball buttons at the top of the app to switch instantly between games. How do I generate a prediction? Select a prediction method (Hot Numbers, Due Numbers, Balanced, or Random) from the dropdown, then tap "Generate prediction". How do I save a prediction? After generating numbers, tap "Save this pick". You can view all your saved picks anytime under the Saved tab. Where do my saved picks go if I delete the app? Saved picks are stored locally on your device only. If you delete the app, this data will be removed and cannot be recovered. What do the stats and frequency numbers mean? The Stats tab shows how often each number and bonus ball has appeared in past draws, based on historical UK draw data. Does this app guarantee winning numbers? No. UK Lottery Predictor is an entertainment and statistical analysis tool only. Lottery draws are random, and no method — including those offered in this app — can predict or guarantee a winning outcome. Is my data shared with anyone? No. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details. © 2026 UK Lottery Predictor.
